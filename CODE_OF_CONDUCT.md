# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders of J² Stack pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of gender, gender identity and expression, sexual orientation, disability, physical appearance, body size, race, age, religion, or technical experience.

## Our Standards

- **Be respectful.** Disagreement is fine — personal attacks are not.
- **Assume good faith.** Most contributions come from a place of trying to help.
- **Give constructive feedback.** Code reviews are about the code, not the person.
- **No harassment.** This includes unwelcome sexual attention, deliberate misgendering, threats, stalking, or any other behaviour that makes people feel unsafe.

## Scope

This code applies to all J² Stack project spaces: GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, and any official communication channels.

## Enforcement

If you experience or witness a violation:

1. **Politely call it out** if you feel safe doing so.
2. **Report it** to the maintainers by email or GitHub DM.
3. If the violation is ongoing or severe, the offending person may be temporarily or permanently banned from project spaces.

Maintainers have the final say and will handle reports confidentially.
